About OKOTU LOGISTICS

Your trusted logistics, clearing & forwarding partner

OKOTU LOGISTICS is a professional logistics, clearing and forwarding company dedicated to helping individuals and businesses move their goods efficiently and with confidence.

We provide reliable logistics solutions, clearing services, freight support and forwarding for local and international shipments. Our goal is to make the movement of goods easier, more efficient and dependable for every customer.

With a commitment to professionalism, transparency and customer satisfaction, we work to deliver a smooth experience from documentation and clearance to the movement and delivery of goods.
